 Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure. 
 On August 17, 2026, HIVE Digital Technologies Ltd. (the "Company") issued a press release announcing that its wholly-owned subsidiary BUZZ High Performance Computing Inc. ("BUZZ") has entered into a five-year GPU cloud services agreement with an enterprise customer having a high investment grade credit profile (the "Customer"). A copy of the press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated into this Item 7.01 by reference.
 The information in this Item 7.01, including Exhibit 99.1, is being furnished and shall not be deemed "filed" for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the "Exchange Act"), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that section, nor shall it be deemed incorporated by reference into any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
 Item 8.01 Other Events. 
 On August 17, 2026, the Company announced that BUZZ entered into a GPU cloud services agreement (the “Agreement’) with the Customer pursuant to which it will provide the Customer with access to a dedicated GPU cluster. The cluster will consist of 2,016 NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra GPUs in NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 rack-scale systems and will be deployed at a facility in Merritt, British Columbia. The cluster will be designed to support large-scale AI training, AI inference and advanced enterprise workloads. Deployment is expected to occur during the fourth quarter of the 2026 calendar year. The estimated total contract value is approximately $350 million over the five-year term. The Company expects capital expenditures associated with the deployment (including the accelerated computing cluster and all associated hardware and service warranties) to total approximately $185 million. The Customer has agreed to furnish a deposit of approximately 10% of the total contract value.
